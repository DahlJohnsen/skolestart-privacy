# Privacy Policy for Skolestart

Last updated: October 2026

## About Skolestart

Skolestart is an educational app designed to help children learn letters, words, reading and basic mathematics.

## Data Collection

Skolestart does not require users to create an account.

Skolestart does not collect names, email addresses or other personal information.

## Analytics

Skolestart uses TelemetryDeck to collect anonymous usage statistics for the purpose of improving the app and identifying problems.

The collected information is anonymous and is not used to identify individual users.

## Advertising

Skolestart does not display third-party advertisements.

## Data Sharing

User data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

## Children's Privacy

Skolestart is intended for children and families. The app is designed to minimize data collection and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
